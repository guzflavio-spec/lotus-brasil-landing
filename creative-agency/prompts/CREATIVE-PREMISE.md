# Creative premise: non-obvious, intelligent, AI-assisted

The team's standard. Applies to every brief, before any concept is shown.

## Principles

1. **Non-obvious.** The obvious idea is a baseline to beat, not a candidate.
2. **Creativity with intelligence.** Every idea carries an insight (a true tension in
   the audience's life or in the product) and a reason it will work. Cleverness
   without an insight is decoration.
3. **AI-assisted.** AI widens the search, attacks weak ideas and prototypes fast.
   People (and the Creative Director role) decide. Never ship an AI first draft as a concept.

## Method (run in this order)

1. **Obvious list.** Write the 10 ideas anyone would have for this brief, including
   what a default AI answer would say. Be honest and fast.
2. **Ban list.** Those 10 are now forbidden, along with their close cousins.
3. **Insight mining.** Write 5 sharp tensions: "People want X but fear/hate/avoid Y".
   Prefer behaviours over demographics. Choose the 2 least discussed.
4. **Forced routes.** Generate ideas through different doors, at least one per door:
   - **Inversion:** do the opposite of the category convention.
   - **Constraint:** one absurd limit (one shot, no words, 3 seconds, one colour).
   - **Borrowed form:** use a format from another world (receipt, weather report,
     field guide, courtroom, nature documentary, postcard, error message).
   - **Zoom:** the smallest real detail of the experience, or the biggest scale of it.
   - **Medium as message:** let the AI tool or the platform itself be part of the idea.
   - **Participation:** the audience completes or changes the idea.
5. **Attack.** For each survivor, argue against it as a hostile client, a bored
   viewer and a competitor. Kill or fix. Record what you killed.
6. **Obviousness score (1-5).** 1 = a competitor would do this tomorrow, 5 = no one
   else would think of it but it is instantly clear. Ship only 4-5, or 3 with a fix plan.
7. **Intelligence check.** Fill in: *Insight / Why it works / How we measure it / Risk.*
   If any blank is empty, the idea is not ready.
8. **Prototype with AI.** Make the cheapest test: a still keyframe, a 5 s clip, a script
   read-through. Judge the prototype, not the description.

## Using AI well

- Use it to **diverge**: ask for 30 bad ideas, then 30 more in a different form.
- Use it as an **adversary**: "Why would this fail? Who has already done this?"
- Use it for **combination**: "Combine route A with constraint B".
- Use it to **produce**: keyframes, clips, voices, mock-ups, captions, variants.
- Do **not** accept the first output. Do **not** accept something that sounds clever
  but has no insight. Do **not** claim facts, numbers or testimonials the AI made up.
- Always keep a human reason for the final pick: "We chose this because ...".

## Anti-patterns (auto-reject)

- Slogans that fit any brand in the category.
- "Authentic stories", "community", "passion", "journey" as the whole idea.
- Trend-jacking with no link to the product.
- A big AI visual with no idea behind it.
- Celebrating the product instead of changing how the audience sees their own situation.
