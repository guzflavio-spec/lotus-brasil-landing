# Template: POV selfie, city, billboard reveal (15 s, 9:16)

Original agency drafts, based on a frame-by-frame analysis of a 15 s vertical ad.
Replace the bracketed variables. Keep the person block identical in all clips.

**Person block:** `[a cheerful woman in her mid-20s, long blonde hair, pink knit sweater]`

## Clip 1: hook (6 s)

```
Handheld selfie-style vertical video, front-facing phone camera at arm's length.
[PERSON BLOCK], wide-eyed and excited, in [CITY SQUARE] at dusk. She gasps, then
points at the glowing "[OFFER TEXT]" billboards behind her. Slight natural handheld
shake. Ambient: crowd noise, no music. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

## Clip 2: whip-pan reveal (5 s)

```
Continuing the same scene: fast whip pan from the woman to a giant billboard showing
her smiling face with the text "[HEADLINE]". Heavy horizontal motion blur at the
midpoint, sharp settle on the billboard. Same city layout, same clothing.
Continuous camera move, no cut, no morph. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

## Clip 3: payoff (5 s)

```
Low-angle shot looking straight up between skyscrapers at a blue dusk sky. [PERSON
BLOCK] leans over the camera lens, hair falling forward, laughing and saying
"[SHORT LINE]". Handheld, wide-angle phone lens. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```
