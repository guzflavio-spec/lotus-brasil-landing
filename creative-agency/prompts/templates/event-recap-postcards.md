# Template: event recap "postcards" (15-20 s, 9:16)

Use real photos from the event as the first frame. Do not generate people.

## Per-photo clip (3-4 s each, 5-6 photos)

```
Animate this photo as a vintage postcard coming to life. Subtle parallax and slow
push-in, gentle handheld sway, soft film grain, warm natural light kept exactly as
in the photo. Faces and clothing stay identical, no morphing, no added text or
logos. 9:16, 4 seconds.
```

## Variations

- **Polaroid drop**: the photo slides in as a polaroid, slight rotation, soft shadow.
- **Postcard flip**: flip to the back with a handwritten line, then flip back.
- **Light leak transition**: exposure blooms to white at the end of the clip, next photo fades in.

## Edit plan

1. Order photos by energy: arrival, connection, peak, wind-down.
2. One music bed, cut on the beat.
3. Opening line (2 s) and closing card with the next event/CTA.
4. Captions on, export 9:16 1080p.

## Caption pattern

`Postcards from [EVENT] in [CITY]. Thank you to everyone who came out. If you missed it,
we might be in your city soon. Link in bio for upcoming events.`
